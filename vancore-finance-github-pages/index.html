TAX HARVESTER

Automated tax preparation

Turn financial records into categorised activity and estimated tax calculations. Tax Harvester is designed to reduce manual sorting and feed the tax buffer with a clearer view of upcoming obligations.

FINANCIAL INDEPENDENCE DAY

Path to financial freedom

Make financial independence a moving target users can understand. The algorithm is intended to update and visualise the projected date when passive portfolio income covers monthly living costs as assets, income and expenses change.

INDEPENDENCE POINT
02

Intelligence that watches, tests and acts

The planned intelligence layer turns the consolidated record into timely signals, simulations and practical actions.

LUMIX

An operator, not just a chatbot

Move from a conversation to a completed task. Lumix is designed to edit financial records, perform calculations, set calendar reminders and run Predict analyses with user approval. Its role is to operate within the platform, not initiate payments.

ASK→REVIEW→APPROVE ACTION
01

Behavioral anomaly detection

Continuously compare spending with the user’s usual patterns. Flag unusual increases in subscriptions or entertainment for review, helping users investigate changes before recurring costs erode their budget.

02

Intelligent financial calendar

A calendar built around cash flow, not birthdays. Connect upcoming obligations with available funds and receive an early warning when a payment could put the financial safety buffer at risk.

03

Financial Simulator Suite

Explore a job loss, promotion, new loan or growing family across 5, 10 and 40 years. Compare the possible impact on net worth and liquidity in a separate simulation environment without changing real account data.

04

Safe Withdrawal Rate testing

Test how retirement withdrawals might hold up through historical bear and bull markets. Portfolio longevity scenarios help users examine when savings could run out and how sensitive a plan is to market conditions.

05

Live tax estimation for B2C and B2B

Planned live estimates of PIT, VAT, Belka capital-gains tax and health insurance contributions for individuals and businesses. Reserve an estimated tax buffer so entrepreneurs and investors can distinguish available cash from money needed for obligations.

06

Alpha signals in the news

Filter news through what the user actually owns. Link portfolio holdings and risk profile to relevant developments and asset sentiment, helping users understand why a story matters to their financial position.

ILLUSTRATIVE WORKFLOW, NOT A USER RESULT

From a spending signal to a better decision

A subscription increase is flagged. The calendar shows how it affects the next payment cycle. Predict explores the longer-term impact, and Lumix can set a review reminder after approval. One record connects detection, context, simulation and action.

Product vision, not a list of verified live services. These capabilities are in development or planned. External data access depends on provider availability, licensing and implementation. Valuations, forecasts and tax figures are estimates, not guaranteed outcomes or personalised financial or tax advice.
